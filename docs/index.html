What we check — and what we do not

Automated checks: exact iPhone model and capacity in the listing title; excluded used/refurbished listings and known refurbishers; HTTPS link validation; detection of recognizable retailer domains; and price source (retailer page versus Google Shopping). We do NOT independently verify third-party seller identity, business registration, customer reviews, whether Amazon itself sells the item, new/sealed condition, Italian warranty, live stock or final delivered cost. Marketplace and unknown-seller prices remain visible but are clearly separated.
