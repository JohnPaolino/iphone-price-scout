How we keep it trustworthy

Searches cover Google Shopping listings for Italy, filtered to exact model and storage. Refurbished, used, accessories, subscriptions, and unknown merchants are excluded. Large retailers that host third-party sellers are placed in a separate review list. A search result cannot prove that a box is factory sealed, and shipping charges are included only when explicitly available.
